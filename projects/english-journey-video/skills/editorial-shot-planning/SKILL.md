# Editorial shot planning — English Journey

## Trigger
Use before a new marketing composition or revision. Read the approved brief and narration first.

## Non-negotiable
- The owner-approved spoken words, order and facts are immutable. Do not shorten or rewrite to match music or visuals.
- The first frame must disclose the intended audience and problem, not a logo or a diary-like anecdote.
- A real screenshot/recording may be called evidence only if it is genuinely captured. Illustrative graphics must not imitate authentic ChatGPT screenshots.
- No guaranteed learning outcomes, fabricated testimonials, or cross-chat memory claims.
- Reuse the existing Remotion composition and source material; this skill does not replace the rendering stack.

## Editorial shotbook
Before coding, make docs/reviews/<slug>/SHOTBOOK.md with one row for each shot:
1. Scene and time range grounded in **actual narration timings**, not imaginary timestamps.
2. Exact narration excerpt, viewer question, and sentence function (hook, pain, solution, proof, benefit, CTA).
3. One takeaway the viewer should understand after seeing it.
4. Visual proof/source or clearly labelled illustration, with attribution or license.
5. One primary on-screen focal element; show change over time instead of static text cards.
6. Voice emphasis, caption emphasis, music/SFX entry point and transition reason.
7. Safe-area, contrast and minimum reading-time risk.
8. Review checkpoint and evidence frame timestamps.

## English Journey Marketing Video 01 anchors
- Hook: within 3 seconds, identify người mất gốc có ChatGPT nhưng không biết học từ đâu.
- Problem: fragmented Grammar/Vocabulary/Speaking.
- Solution: visibly arrange into Reading & Grammar, Vocabulary and Error Log.
- Proof: original Lesson 15 sentence, spelling of supermarke/yesteday, missing and, corrected sentence held legibly.
- Benefit: errors recorded for a future targeted review, no automatic syncing claim.
- CTA: only one action, bình luận BẮT ĐẦU.

## Gate
Reject shotbook if a cold viewer cannot identify the problem in the first 3 seconds, if the demo becomes the whole story, if the evidence is faked, or if the approved words changed. Record problems and repair the storyboard before rendering.
