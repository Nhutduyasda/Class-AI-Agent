# Marketing Short Story — English Journey

This skill applies to awareness/marketing videos. A study-journal montage alone is NOT a marketing result.

## Six-step framework
1. **HOOK (0–3s):** name the audience and their everyday frustration in a question or concrete claim. The first on-screen frame must be understood by a new viewer.
2. **PROBLEM:** show consequences of scattered learning, not just the creator's timeline.
3. **SOLUTION:** introduce English Journey as a structured *personal* ChatGPT learning workflow, never an official OpenAI product.
4. **DEMO:** prove one concrete feature from genuine learning material; all text must match evidence. Animate WHAT went wrong and HOW it was addressed.
5. **BENEFIT:** explain precisely how an Error Log/targeted review changes the next study step. Never imply automatic synchronization unless verified in product.
6. **CTA:** invite one low-friction next action with honest follow-through. No fake urgency, guaranteed results or invented testimonials.

## Voice & picture
Each spoken line must have a visible teaching/job cue. Avoid long text walls. Build a semantic storyboard BEFORE motion. Use short conversational Vietnamese clauses for narration and restrained visuals/music. Match frame timings to voice. Check muted playback comprehension and single-view comprehension.

## Copy lock rule
When an owner APPROVES a script, narration changes require explicit approval; timing, visual emphasis and subtitle segmentation may vary without altering words or claims. For Video 01, read `briefs/marketing-video-01-approved.json` and `docs/MARKETING_VIDEO_01_APPROVED_SCRIPT.md`. An agent must NOT replace the marketer-approved message with a personal learning diary.

## Reusable template for other videos
Produce one metadata brief with target audience, ONE pain, ONE promise, truth-backed demo, ONE benefit and ONE CTA. New topics require separate scripts and user approval before render.

## Deliverable criteria
Answer after watching the video once: Who is this for? What is the problem? How does English Journey help? What proof was shown? What should I do next? If any are ambiguous, fail editorial QA even if video passes ffprobe.
