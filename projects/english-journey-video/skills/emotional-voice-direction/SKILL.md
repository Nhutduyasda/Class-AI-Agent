# Emotional Voice Direction — English Journey
## Non-negotiable
Natural performance precedes text-to-speech provider. A monotonous voice with background music remains monotonous.
1. Write an authentic first-person micro-story in Vietnamese, spoken language not slide-reading. Only describe a real learner mistake; no inflated claims.
2. Make a performance sheet for each line: intent [surprise, mild embarrassment, discovery, reassurance], key stressed words, breath/pause, expected seconds, pronunciation of English inserts.
3. Record two or more **permitted** voice takes (either human voice or TTS), choose after *listening to the exported audio*. Never claim an emotional TTS tag works unless the provider/model supports and demonstrates it.
4. Prefer a natural user recording if supplied; do not synthesize the user's voice without consent. If only Edge TTS available, test expressive pacing and shorter lines but do not mislabel as emotionally rich.
5. Use a separate native English voice only if genuinely required for a short example; no abrupt switch mid-Vietnamese sentence. On screen do most of the English teaching through highlighted text, not through lengthy English VO.
6. No silent automatic failover to an unlicensed service. Before paid or commercial usage check provider's commercial terms and get user's permission for charges.
7. If voice remains synthetic or flat after two auditions, report NEEDS_HUMAN_VOICE, offer a self-recorded voice track, and DO NOT label ready to publish.
8. Keep waveform/audio available for manual review and record actual reviewed file hash. Listening is distinct from ASR transcription.
## Voice acceptance
Clear 0–3 s hook, emotional contour in problem→reveal→relief, conversational Vietnamese, purposeful half-beat pause before reveal, no robotic cadence, music never obscures speech. User has final veto.
