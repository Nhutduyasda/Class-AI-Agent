# Acceptance — English Journey video review agent

## Objective, automatically checked
- [ ] Candidate MP4 exists, ffprobe decodes metadata; 1080x1920, 30 FPS, H.264 and AAC track.
- [ ] Duration and nonempty video/audio streams are checked.
- [ ] Six stage boundaries and exact approved spoken words are checked when timeline and approved brief are supplied.
- [ ] Proof frames from real MP4: opening frame, 1s, 2.5s, hook/problem/solution/demo/benefit/CTA, and near end.
- [ ] Short audio clips captured for external listening.
- [ ] Unit tests for metadata, scene/copy checks and pairwise consensus pass.

## Editorial, require actual perception
- [ ] Cold viewer understands problem/target user within 3s.
- [ ] HOOK -> PROBLEM -> SOLUTION -> DEMO -> BENEFIT -> CTA is clear even to first-time viewers.
- [ ] Lesson 15 correction is only evidence; not a personal diary.
- [ ] Vietnam-language voice sounds natural and intentional; any English words are pronounceable.
- [ ] Background music audible but not overpowering and licensed for marketing.
- [ ] Typography legible on phone and doesn't collide with TikTok overlays.
- [ ] Final CTA is one clear invitation to comment BẮT ĐẦU.
- [ ] A/B evaluation documented in two reverse-order passes; no invented reviewer results.
- [ ] Rights of the voice, score, artwork documented.

**Release condition:** owner APPROVES explicitly. Technical PASS, AI preference or high subjective score alone must NEVER mark READY TO POST.
