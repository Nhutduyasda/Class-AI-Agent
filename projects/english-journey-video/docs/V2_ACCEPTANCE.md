# V2 Human-first acceptance checklist
The last version passed technical sync but **FAILED** user evaluation for engagement and communication. Recreating the old video plus music is not sufficient.

## Pass/Fail gates
- [ ] A stranger understands WHAT was wrong and HOW it was corrected after one normal-speed watch.
- [ ] Hook conveys the learner pain and concrete mistake in <=3 s.
- [ ] Vietnamese narrator sounds human and engaged, not monotone. Audition at least two licensed/consented takes, **listen** to the full exported audio.
- [ ] Music actually exists in the MP4, fits tone, fades cleanly and doesn't mask voice. Store license proof or original-synthesis description.
- [ ] All English on screen is proofread; BEFORE kept exactly intentional, AFTER exact approved sentence.
- [ ] Show spelling edits and connector "and" visually, rather than merely naming them in speech.
- [ ] Subtitles match actual audio timing; Vietnamese diacritics and safe areas checked; export is understandable with sound OFF.
- [ ] Render 1080x1920 at 30 fps; H264 + AAC audio. Probe/inspect listenable final.
- [ ] Five contact/proof frames at opening, mistakes, bridge, full fix, CTA.
- [ ] Quality rated separately for hook, speech, music, comprehension, visual, mobile readability (1–5 scale), and score must be >=4 for each by a human reviewer.
- [ ] No external auto-post; user explicitly approves.
A machine cannot certify a human emotional response. Any missing human listening or reviewer approval must be marked BLOCKED, not PASS.
