# English Journey Lesson 15 — V2 Creative Brief
**Status:** concept for production and human approval; no claim V2 already rendered.
**Audience:** Vietnamese beginner English learners who fail to turn corrected answers into active writing.
**One sentence takeaway:** Mình không chỉ nhờ AI sửa lỗi; mình hiểu và viết lại câu đó.
**Voice:** first-person personal journal, curious and warm, modest emotional movement. Avoid a generic motivational AI pitch.
**Opening hook:** "Một câu tiếng Anh thôi… mà tôi sai tận ba chỗ." (Check exact count with English QA, revise to "mấy chỗ" if ambiguity; distinguish spelling x2 + missing connector.)
**Verified original:** `After work I went to the supermarke bought food yesteday`
**Verified retry:** `After work yesterday, I went to the supermarket and bought food for dinner.`
**Suggested 32–39s narrative (adapt to real narration timing):**
- 0–3s: emotional hook with original error card; music low and warm.
- 3–8s: "Đây là câu tôi viết khi kể chuyện đi siêu thị hôm qua. Nhìn thì ổn, nhưng..." show intentional original.
- 8–16s: Zoom on `supermarke` and `yesteday`; simple spelling highlights; micro sound cue.
- 16–23s: "Quan trọng hơn, tôi quên chữ **and** để nối hai hành động: went ... and bought." Visually animate connecting line.
- 23–32s: Correction animates in three simple moves. `After work yesterday, I went to the supermarket and bought food for dinner.` Hold full corrected sentence long enough to read.
- 32–39s: "AI giải thích. Nhưng chính tôi phải viết lại. Đó là cách tôi đang học." Small CTA "Bạn từng sai lỗi này chưa?"
**Narrative limitations:** This is a pilot story from the project. Do not claim TOEIC score or guaranteed method effectiveness. Do not fabricate screenshot, an AI chat response, outcomes, or a person saying they used the product.
**Style:** keep brand dark green/mint but replace static slide stack with a teaching visual metaphor: wrong words crossed out → `and` physically bridges two actions → completed sentence. Use purposeful motion and pauses. Use restrained background bed, optional sound cues and a voice performance with warm dynamics.
**QA gates:** first 3 seconds comprehensible, one lesson, emotional voice assessed by listening, exact English strings, a licensed/original background bed, no music masking, clearly readable muted, 1080x1920 MP4 with AAC audio; human approval needed.
